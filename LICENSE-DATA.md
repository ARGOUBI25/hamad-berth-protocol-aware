# Licence of data and result files

The instance files (`hamad_instance.json`, `hamad_instance_20250516.json`), the CSV/JSON/TeX
files under `sensitivity_output/`, `baselines_16mai/`, `baselines_29mai/` and the figures
under `figures/` are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The instances are *synthetic, calibrated* instances (see `metadata.instance_type` in the JSON files).
They use aggregate vessel-call counts from the public HDX "Qatar Daily Port Activity"
dataset and throughput figures published by Mwani Qatar. No vessel-level record of a real call
is reproduced: vessel identities, arrival/departure times and container flows are generated
(Section 4 of the paper). The upstream sources keep their own terms of use.
